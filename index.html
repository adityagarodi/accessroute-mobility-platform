How It Works

Four steps to every accessible journey

AccessRoute combines real-time infrastructure data, community reports, and your personal accessibility profile to guide you every step of the way.

1
Set Your Profile
Enter your accessibility needs once. We remember wheelchair requirements, elevator preference, low-floor buses, and more.
2
Plan Your Route
Enter destination. We calculate multiple accessible options ranked by accessibility score, ETA, and walking distance.
3
Live Monitoring
Infrastructure changes are tracked in real time — elevators, ramps, accessible toilets, low-floor bus arrivals.
4
Auto Rerouting
When a facility fails mid-journey, AccessRoute instantly recalculates an alternative accessible route and notifies you.
Features

Built for real-world accessibility

Every feature is designed with accessibility-first principles, WCAG-compliant design, and real operational challenges in mind.

Interactive Accessible Map

Live OpenStreetMap view showing elevators, ramps, accessible toilets, bus stops, stations, and verified facility statuses.

Smart Route Planning

Compare routes by accessibility score, ETA, walking distance, and transfer count. Every route detail is presented clearly.

Automatic Rerouting

When infrastructure fails — elevator down, ramp blocked — the system instantly detects the impact and recalculates your route.

Community Verification

Community reports for real-world conditions are clearly labelled and separated from official verified data.

Voice Guidance

Browser Web Speech API delivers turn-by-turn voice guidance with multilingual support: English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Personalised Profile

Save your accessibility requirements once. Routes, facility filters, and alerts all adapt to your personal preferences automatically.

Accessibility First

Designed for every ability

AccessRoute is built with WCAG-conscious color contrast, keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support throughout.

  • Wheelchair accessible route planning
  • Voice guidance via Web Speech API
  • High contrast and large font modes
  • Reduced motion option for vestibular sensitivity
  • Screen reader optimised with semantic HTML & ARIA
  • English, Hindi & Marathi language support
